# Appendix: Competitor analysis

*Researched September 2026. Prices and features change fast — re-check before quoting anything publicly.*

## The market in one line

"Cursor for Product Management" is now a named category — YC listed it in its Spring 2026 Request for Startups. The space is getting crowded, but almost every player owns **one slice** of product work. Nobody owns the whole loop:

**listen → understand → decide → plan → execute → remember**

That loop is Selixa's positioning.

## The five groups we compete with

### 1. Meeting note takers
**Granola, Otter, Fireflies, tl;dv, Read.ai, Fathom**

- Strong at: capturing meetings, summaries, action items. Granola is bot-free and loved by founders (Free / $14 / $35 per user per month).
- Weak at: anything after the meeting. Each meeting is an island. They don't know your roadmap, analytics or feedback, and they don't recommend what to build.
- **Our angle:** "Other tools take notes. Selixa takes it from there."

### 2. AI spec / PRD writers
**ChatPRD, general chat assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)**

- Strong at: fast drafts of PRDs, user stories, strategy docs. ChatPRD has PM-specific templates and coaching.
- Weak at: context. You bring the evidence by copy-pasting. Light integrations (ChatPRD mostly relies on copy-paste into Jira/Linear). No memory of past decisions across the product.
- **Our angle:** "Every answer is grounded in your product's actual context" (section 04) and "Don't just ask. Work." (section 05).

### 3. Feedback & insight platforms
**Productboard (Spark), BuildBetter, Enterpret, Dovetail, Unwrap**

- Strong at: clustering customer feedback into themes, citing quotes, generating specs from evidence. Productboard Spark works well if everything already lives in Productboard. BuildBetter pulls from calls, support tools and Slack.
- Weak at: built for larger product orgs, with heavy setup and hierarchy. Insight-first, not decision- or execution-first. Not made for solo founders or people running several products.
- **Our angle:** lean teams and founders, proactive nudges (section 09), and multiple products (section 07).

### 4. Roadmap & planning tools
**Jira Product Discovery, Aha!, airfocus, Linear, Notion (+ Notion AI)**

- Strong at: storing roadmaps, tasks and docs. Linear and Notion are adding AI agents on top of their own data.
- Weak at: they're systems of record, not systems of thinking. They hold what you already decided; they don't tell you what to decide, or why.
- **Our angle:** integrate with these, don't replace them. "Bring your product context with you" (section 11). Selixa decides; Linear and Jira track.

### 5. Coding tools used by PMs
**Cursor, Claude Code, v0, Lovable, Bolt**

- Strong at: prototyping and shipping small changes. PMs already use Cursor to read codebases and draft specs.
- Weak at: they understand code, not customers, meetings or the market.
- **Our angle:** borrow the *feeling* of Cursor (an AI that works inside your context), applied to product decisions instead of code.

**The three things only Selixa should claim:**

1. **Proactive.** It notices things before you ask (section 09).
2. **Closes the loop.** From the conversation all the way to the outcome (sections 03 → 10).
3. **One brain, many products.** Built for founders running more than one thing (section 07).

## Risks to watch

- **Platform creep.** Notion, Linear and Productboard are all shipping AI agents. Win on *cross-tool* context and *proactivity*, not on any single feature.
- **"Just ChatGPT with docs."** The biggest objection. Section 05 must show evidence-backed reasoning (numbers, sources, next steps), never a generic chat reply.
- **Granola's love among founders.** Don't fight it on note-taking quality. Position meetings as the *input*, not the product.
- **Honesty on the site.** No competitor names, logos or ✗ marks on public pages. Compare against categories ("note takers", "chat assistants"). Every claim in the comparison table must be true of the shipped product.

## Messaging to use / avoid

**Use:**
- "Other tools capture the conversation. Selixa carries it forward."
- "You already have tools. You don't have a product manager."
- "Grounded in your product, not the internet."

**Avoid:**
- "AI note taker" (puts us in the Granola/Otter bucket)
- "PRD generator" (puts us in the ChatPRD bucket)
- "Feedback analytics" (puts us in the Productboard/Enterpret bucket)
